The short version

Senior data scientist in Singapore Airlines' Information Technology Division. I lead end-to-end development and deployment of customer-facing and internal GenAI applications: agent harnesses with code execution and graph-based memory, retrieval infrastructure for the public chatbot, and the evaluation systems that keep them trustworthy.

BA in Computer Science and Linguistics from UC Berkeley, 4.0 GPA, Dean's List. My NLP work started with a 2017 IEEE TENCON paper on punctuation prediction, continued through gesture-annotation research in the Linguistics Research Apprenticeship Practicum, and carried into document pipelines for nuclear science literature at NucScholar.

I advise the Group CEO through Singapore Airlines' reverse-mentoring programme, mentor interns who ship production features, and have emceed the company's annual business meeting. Trilingual in English, Chinese, and Japanese. Outside work: singing, guitar, games, hiking, and brewing coffee.
