About

A little about me

I'm Char Juin — a senior data scientist at Singapore Airlines, where I own the end-to-end development and deployment of multiple customer-facing and internal GenAI applications. My work spans the full stack of applied AI: agent harnesses with code execution and graph-based memory, retrieval infrastructure that keeps public chatbots fast and accurate, and the evaluation systems that keep all of it trustworthy.

I studied Computer Science and Linguistics at UC Berkeley (BA, 4.0 GPA, Dean's List). My path into NLP started with a 2017 IEEE TENCON paper on punctuation prediction, continued through gesture-annotation research in Berkeley's Linguistics Research Apprenticeship Practicum, and carried on into building document pipelines for nuclear science literature at NucScholar.

Beyond engineering I like to lead — I advise Singapore Airlines' Group CEO as part of a reverse-mentoring programme, mentor interns who ship production features, and have emceed the company's annual business meeting. I'm trilingual (English, Chinese, Japanese); in my free time I enjoy running, hiking, brewing coffee, playing guitar, and tinkering with a personal Kubernetes cluster and open-weight models.
